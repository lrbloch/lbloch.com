About

I sit where business needs, user experience, and engineering reality meet — most useful when the problem is technical enough that someone has to understand how it actually works.

My path runs from firmware and hardware-adjacent engineering into product leadership. I began as a computer engineer and technical consultant — home security and automation at Alarm.com, then insurance and data governance — before a deep interest in technology's role in education pulled me toward learning products.

That took me to the Teachers College DLL Lab and HOMER by BEGiN in children's edtech, and to Safelite AutoGlass, where I lead technical product work spanning customer experience and applied AI. Across all of it, I keep the same bias: products should earn their complexity, not add it for its own sake.

I care about increasing minority representation in STEM — the mission behind ArcHoops, the basketball-analytics edtech venture I co-founded.
